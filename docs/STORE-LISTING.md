# CareCircle — store listing

The customer-facing page: what a family reads before they decide to add it. Amazon's
Add-on certification requires a tagline, description, sample utterances, and a privacy
statement; this is that, written in the customer's language, not the engineer's.

Everything here is true of what is built. Nothing describes a feature that is not live.

---

## Tagline

**Care is already shared. Now the load is too.**

(Alternate, shorter: *"Nobody drops the ball, because nobody carries it alone."*)

## One-line

CareCircle keeps track of what your family needs to do for someone you love — and makes
sure every job has a name on it, before something quietly falls through.

## Description

When a parent needs looking after, the work gets split across a family — a daughter, a
son abroad, a nurse who visits, a neighbour who helps. Everyone does their part, and yet
the thing that gets missed is the thing **nobody realised was theirs**: the ride to the
clinic no one booked, the evening tablet no one checked, the prescription that ran out on
a day no one was watching.

CareCircle listens to the ordinary signals of care — a calendar invite, an email from the
clinic, someone saying "I gave her the heart pill" — and turns them into a shared picture
of what needs doing and who has it. When something has no owner, it says so, and it asks
the right person to take it on. It never assigns work behind your back, and it never tells
you someone "missed" their medicine when all it really knows is that nothing was written
down. It says what is true, names who said it, and leaves the deciding to your family.

It works by voice, on a shared screen, and quietly in the background — so the person being
cared for stays a participant in their own life, not a case being managed.

## Who it's for

- **Families caring for an ageing parent** — especially when the people who love them are
  spread across cities or countries. A daughter in Lagos and a son in London can hold one
  shared picture of how their mother's week is going, in her own timezone, without a group
  chat that nobody reads.
- **Anyone supporting a person with a disability** — where the difference between "you took
  your medicine" and "your aide said you took your medicine" matters, and being believed
  about your own life is not a small thing.
- **A paid carer and the family who employ them** — each sees exactly what their role needs
  and nothing it doesn't.

CareCircle was built by a Nigerian engineer with diaspora families in mind first: the
coordination problem is hardest, and the stakes highest, exactly where the family is not
in one house or one country.

## The privacy promise (say this plainly, it sells)

This is your family's health information. So:

- **We only ever tell you what was actually recorded.** CareCircle never claims a dose
  was missed, or a job was skipped — an absent record is not evidence, and it will not be
  spoken as if it were.
- **We always say who said something.** If a carer logged a dose on your behalf, it says
  so — it never passes off someone else's account of you as your own word.
- **Nobody is assigned work without agreeing to it.** CareCircle asks; a person accepts.
  It cannot put a job in your name while you sleep.
- **Each person sees only what their role needs.** A visiting carer sees their shift, not
  the family's private business.
- **You can see why.** Every prompt, every ranking, every decision the system makes on its
  own can be traced back to the record it came from.

## Category & positioning (for the submission, not the customer)

- MCP Toolkit add-on (care coordination is not an existing Alexa+ category).
- Sold as a family subscription, not per-seat — the value is the shared picture, and
  charging each carer would punish exactly the families with the most people helping.
